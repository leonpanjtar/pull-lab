## What repeats (Claude, 2026-11-20)

- 11 of 12 sell a custom outdoor build to homeowners. Hrovat is the only one with a distributor angle, Caribbean the only dealer.
- Every one quoted by hand before: 30 minutes (YourPergola) to 3 hours (Spa Solutions) a quote, often one person (Impol).
- The buyer-side failure is speed: buyers go to whoever prices first (YourPergola, Mavlli, Nordin) or ask outside office hours (Armat).
- 4 of 12 were new brands entering a market against established sellers (Garden Spirit, Nordin, Mavlli, FollHaus). Their stories have the clearest Unavoidable: no showroom or reputation, so the first priced quote is the only way to win.
- 6 of 12 pair the configurator with paid Meta ads. Spolding says the tool alone didn't work until the funnel existed.
- Product type doesn't separate them. Pergolas (5), modular/garden rooms (3), fences (2), carports (1), blinds (1).
- 4 of the 12 featured customers (Caribbean Blinds, Hrovat, Impol, Dekor senčila) show as dormant or quiet at €0 MRR in SQ Hive. Could be a tenant mapping gap; worth a check before using them in outbound.
