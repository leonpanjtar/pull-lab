## 1. The bottleneck: buyers who pull get closed as "wrong timing"

I might be missing context, but this is the clearest pattern in the data. The case study filter works: 4 of 4 v1 fits pulled this week, 0 of 2 non-fits did. What fails is the step after. Marcos at Homestead Millworks asked for pricing in writing and a demo link, said the budget was there, and named a 30-day to 3-month window. He was marked lost an hour later. Across the quarter, "wrong timing" is the top loss reason, and 11 of those 19 were at Commited or Confirmed.

The same gap blocks the case study. The why-now that showed up this week, losing the configurator you quote with (My Retreat's supplier stopped manufacturing, Homestead's supplier simulator ends), sits in a rep's note and an AI summary, never in the buyer's words.


**The move:** a v1 buyer who asks for price, a link or a start date stays open with a dated next step, never "wrong timing". On every v1 call, ask "What happens if this isn't live by then?" and paste the answer word for word into Pipedrive.
